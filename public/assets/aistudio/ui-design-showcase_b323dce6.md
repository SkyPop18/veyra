# VEYRA — Visual UI/UX Design Showcase

Here are the high-fidelity design mockups generated for **Veyra**, capturing every screen and component before we begin implementation.

---

## 1. Landing Page (`/`)
*The public front door with animated live ticker, hero statement, 3D glass showcase, and feature highlights.*

![Veyra Landing Page Showcase](/C:/Users/Akshat/.gemini/antigravity/brain/a8960315-fd88-41df-93b6-5c440ac59211/veyra_landing_page_mockup_png_1795338864127.jpg)

**Key Sections:**
- **Sleek Navbar**: Minimalist brand typography with subtle blue indicator, links to Currencies, Crypto, Leaderboard, News, and CTA buttons.
- **Real-time Marquee Ticker**: Continuous live ticker displaying benchmark fiat and crypto rates.
- **Hero Title**: *"Track Every Currency. Analyze Every Move."* with electric blue glow backdrop.
- **3D Frosted Glass Showcase**: Floating preview displaying live charts, sparklines, and strength indicators.
- **Feature Cards**: Frosted blur cards covering Currency Strength Index, Personalized Feeds, and AI Market Insights.

## 2. Dashboard Home (`/dashboard/home`)
*The primary daily workstation featuring followed currency strength bar charts, quick stats, and the 4-quadrant news grid.*

![Veyra Dashboard Home](/C:/Users/Akshat/.gemini/antigravity/brain/a8960315-fd88-41df-93b6-5c440ac59211/veyra_dashboard_home_mockup_1795338862600.jpg)

**Key Sections:**
- **Navigation Sidebar**: Clean icons for Home, Leaderboard, Currencies, Crypto, Compare, News, Watchlists, Alerts, and Settings.
- **Row 1 (Quick Stats)**:
  - *Fear & Greed Sentiment Dial* (Real-time gauge from Alternative.me)
  - *Top Mover Today* (with percentage change and sparkline)
  - *Benchmark Asset* (BTC/USD live price & volume)
  - *Platform Health / Currencies Monitored*
- **Row 2 (Followed Currencies Strength Bar Graph)**:
  - Vertical bar comparison widget plotting user's followed currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, BTC, ETH, JPY, INR) on a normalized 0–100 strength scale.
- **Row 3 (4-Card News Grid)**:
  - **Economic News**: Central bank policies, interest rate updates, GDP indices.
  - **Global Tax Updates**: Cross-border trade tariffs, crypto taxation policies.
  - **Market News**: Real-time forex flows and cryptocurrency market movements.
  - **Suggested For You (AI-Curated)**: Recommendations based on your search history and mood, featuring inline [Like] and [Save] buttons.

## 3. Currency Strength Leaderboard & Head-to-Head Compare (`/leaderboard` & `/compare`)
*Identify which currency is currently dominating the global market.*

![Currency Strength Leaderboard & Compare](/C:/Users/Akshat/.gemini/antigravity/brain/a8960315-fd88-41df-93b6-5c440ac59211/veyra_currency_strength_dashboard_1795338858393.jpg)

**Key Sections:**
- **Head-to-Head Currency Comparison Widget**:
  - Dual currency selection: e.g. **EUR vs USD**.
  - Strength metrics side-by-side with comparative delta badge (*"USD is +10.5% stronger this week"*).
  - TradingView-style performance line chart comparing rising/falling trends.
- **Ranked Leaderboard Table**:
  - Switchable tabs for **Fiat Currencies** and **Cryptocurrencies**.
  - Rank (#1, #2, #3...), Flag/Asset icon, Strength Score (0–100 blue gradient progress bar).
  - 24h & 7d change columns with micro-sparklines and direct 'Compare' actions.

*Clean, frosted authentication with Google One-Tap and 6-digit Email OTP verification.*

![Auth and OTP Modal](/C:/Users/Akshat/.gemini/antigravity/brain/a8960315-fd88-41df-93b6-5c440ac59211/veyra_auth_modal_mockup_1795338857613.jpg)
